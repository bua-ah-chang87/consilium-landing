Before you start

How Scriba fits
into your practice.

Scriba runs on its own

Scriba is a standalone platform. It does not read from, or write to, your CRM, planning software or any other practice system, and nothing you enter is automatically synchronised with them.

What that means in practice:

  • Information in: paste meeting notes, upload a fact-find PDF, or type into the fields directly.
  • Work out: export a draft to Word (.docx), or print it to PDF.
  • Inside Scriba: drafts are saved as versions you can restore, and each module holds the information you enter into it.

If your workflow depends on data flowing automatically between Scriba and another system, please raise it on a discovery call before subscribing.

Client data and privacy

A short summary of what our policies say. They are the full and governing statement — please read them before entering client information.

  • AI provider: Scriba uses Anthropic's Claude API. The information you enter is sent to that API to generate drafts, compliance findings and extracted data.
  • Model training: our policies state that your inputs are not used by Consilium Systems or Anthropic to train AI models without your explicit consent.
  • Storage and location: data is stored using Supabase, with hosting on Vercel. Our Privacy Policy states that these providers may store or process data outside Australia, and that you consent to that overseas disclosure by using Scriba.
  • Access and retention: the Privacy Policy describes row-level security so users access only their own data, encryption in transit and at rest, and deletion or de-identification of personal information within a reasonable timeframe after an account is closed.
  • Sensitive information: both policies ask you not to enter sensitive personal information beyond what is reasonably necessary for the output you are producing.

Read the full Privacy Policy, AI Usage Policy and Terms of Service. You remain responsible for handling client information in line with your own privacy and AFSL obligations.
